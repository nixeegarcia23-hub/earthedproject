Where the donations went

We have partnered with a public school in the island of Negros Occidental: Jose D. Cuaycong Elementary School. The school serves 145 children, whose families have an average household income of less than $130 a month. It has been serving its community since the 1940s. For decades, it has relied on government funding. Unfortunately, corruption and the misuse of public funds are a well-known and deeply frustrating reality in the Philippines.

That's why your donations are incredibly appreciated! Your generosity allows us to do something tangible for these children.
